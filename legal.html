We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The site works with screen readers and keyboard navigation, supports your device's text-size and dark-mode settings, and respects reduced-motion settings.

Allergen notice

Please tell us about any food allergy before you order, every time, including when you order through a delivery app.

Our kitchen prepares food that contains or may contain these major allergens:

  • Soy (soy sauce, marinades and many sauces)
  • Wheat and gluten (ramen noodles, soy sauce, dumplings, egg rolls, tempura and breaded items)
  • Egg (egg toppings, egg rolls and some sauces)
  • Shellfish (shrimp, crab and octopus)
  • Fish (poke bowls)
  • Sesame (sesame seeds and sesame oil)
  • Milk (may be used in cream croquettes and some sauces)
  • Other allergens may be used in sauces, marinades and fried foods. Ask us about any specific dish.

All our food is made in one shared kitchen, using shared equipment and fryers. Because of this, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free of any allergen. Descriptions such as "vegetarian" or "vegan" refer to the ingredients only and do not rule out cross-contact.
